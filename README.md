## Featured Research

### Spatial Analysis of Bilharzia Prevalence in Coastal Kenya

My academic research investigates geographical patterns and factors associated with bilharzia prevalence among children aged 9–15 years in coastal Kenya.

**Methods of interest:** Spatial autocorrelation, Moran's I, prevalence mapping and statistical modelling.

**Tools:** R, sf, spdep, ggplot2, and data visualisation tools.
